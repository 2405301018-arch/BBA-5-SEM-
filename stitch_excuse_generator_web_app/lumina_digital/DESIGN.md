# Lumina Digital Design System

## Brand & Style
Lumina Digital is a forward-thinking, high-tech brand that emphasizes clarity, innovation, and digital-first experiences. The brand personality is vibrant and energetic, moving away from earthy tones toward a tech-focused "Vibrant Modern" aesthetic. We evoke a sense of precision and speed, utilizing high-contrast elements and a dark-mode default to create a premium, immersive environment. The target audience is tech-savvy professionals who value efficient, sleek, and visually stimulating interfaces.

## Colors
The palette is centered around a deep, tech-inspired dark mode. 
* **Primary:** Sky Blue (#7dd3fc) - used for primary actions and brand presence.
* **Secondary:** Steel Blue (#88b4cc) - used for secondary actions and supporting information.
* **Tertiary:** Soft Lavender (#c8a0f0) - used for accents, highlights, and specialized data points.
* **Neutral:** Midnight Navy (#1a2438) - the foundation for backgrounds and surfaces.

The system utilizes a **vibrant** color variant to ensure high energy and clear contrast against the dark background.

## Typography
The system uses **Inter** across all roles to achieve a clean, Swiss-style precision.
* **Headlines:** Set in Inter with bold weights for clear hierarchy.
* **Body:** Set in Inter with standard weights, optimized for long-form reading on screens.
* **Labels:** Set in Inter with medium weights and slight letter spacing for utility-focused text.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column grid system designed for responsiveness. We use an 8px base unit (Spacing: 2) for all spatial relationships. 
* **Gutter:** 16px.
* **Margins:** 24px on desktop, 16px on mobile.
* Vertical spacing follows a consistent rhythm of 8px, 16px, 24px, and 48px increments.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through tonal layering. In this dark-themed environment, higher elevation levels are represented by progressively lighter shades of the Midnight Navy neutral color. Subtle 1px borders and occasional primary-tinted glows provide definition without clutter.

## Shapes
The shape language is **Rounded**. 
* **Standard components (Buttons, Inputs):** 8px (0.5rem) border radius.
* **Large components (Cards, Modals):** 16px (1rem) border radius.
This moderate rounding provides a sophisticated, modern feel that balances the precision of the typography.

## Components
* **Buttons:** Primary buttons are Sky Blue with dark text; secondary buttons use a Steel Blue outline.
* **Inputs:** Feature a dark background with a Sky Blue focus ring.
* **Cards:** Elevated navy surfaces with 16px rounded corners.
* **Chips/Badges:** Utilize the Soft Lavender tertiary color for high-visibility tagging.
* **Lists:** Clean, scanable rows with subtle neutral-toned dividers.
